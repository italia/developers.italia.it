Confirmation emails with calendar files, and reminders

Jitsi Meet room embedded in the portal, with a waiting room and a device check

Chat, Q&A with upvotes, polls, word cloud, reactions and a raised-hand queue

Moderators and speakers join through a magic link, without an account

Control of participants' microphones and cameras, including giving the floor

Video recording, optionally with one audio track per participant, with their consent

Video library, event analytics, pre-event and post-event questionnaires

Optional AI post-production inside the cluster, with no external APIs

Subtitles, transcripts, summaries, translations and dubbing of recordings

    Extended description

    PA Webinar is a web platform that public administrations use to run public online events (webinars, presentations, open meetings) on their own infrastructure. It uses self-hosted Jitsi Meet as its audio and video engine and the .italia design system (Bootstrap Italia and design-react-kit) for the interface, which is available in the 24 official languages of the European Union. Each event has a public page, registration with consents that are never pre-ticked, and a room that opens and closes on schedule. In the room the audience takes part through chat, Q&A with upvotes, polls, word clouds, reactions and raised hands; moderators and speakers join with a link and need no account. After the event the recording is published to the video library. The optional AI post-production, which runs inside the cluster, adds subtitles, a transcript, a summary, translations and dubbing. It installs on Kubernetes with a single Helm chart, from one k3s server to a managed cluster. A conference stays on one bridge (Jitsi Videobridge). More bridges carry more conferences and, in the full profile, they start before scheduled events and scale to zero between them. Branding, offered languages and registration mode are configured at runtime, without a rebuild.
